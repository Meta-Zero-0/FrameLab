What to try and what this proves

Turn lamp output or reflectance to zero: fill disappears. Rotate the board away: fill disappears. Move a blocker across either path: fill decreases or becomes uneven. Increase sampling to check whether the pattern stabilises. Brightness is fixed, so darkening is not cancelled by automatic exposure.

The readout measures CPU calculation time, not GPU frame time. This pilot uses an ideal diffuse white board, point spotlight and opaque box blockers. The receiver is a virtual measurement plane, not a solid subject. It does not yet support arbitrary models, silver/gold materials, further bounces, or a path-traced reference. Coarse samples can cause banding near blockers. Not a calibrated exposure prediction.
